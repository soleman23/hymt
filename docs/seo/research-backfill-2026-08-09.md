# HYMT launch content — researched backfill

Prepared for `www.hymtravel.com` on **August 9, 2026**.

## Status and publishing rule

This is a research-assisted working draft covering the researchable launch material: provisional luxury planning ranges, inclusions/exclusions, cost drivers, authority links, editorial “Best For” labels, and published flight durations. Ranges are presented as editorial planning bands assembled from published sources — not personal planning figures and not live quotes.

## Cost methodology used for provisional ranges

Every researched range below uses the following normalization:

- USD, per the basis stated in the original brief.
- Two adults sharing one room/cabin.
- Five-star or boutique-luxury accommodation, private transfers, and privately guided touring where those are normal for the destination.
- Shoulder-season travel; peak/festive periods are excluded and named as a driver.
- International airfare, travel insurance, discretionary shopping, and gratuities are excluded unless a row expressly says otherwise.
- HYMT client planning fee: **$0**, based on the existing HYMT business record; supplier and cancellation terms still vary.
- Ranges are editorial planning bands assembled from published offerings and official fee/tax sources, not live quotes or guarantees.
- Every perishable figure is marked **verified August 9, 2026**.

### Cost-range quality gate

All **54 of 54** rows contain a low–high band, inclusions, exclusions, drivers, evidence, confidence, and a verification date. Evidence strength is:

| Confidence tier | Rows | Publishing treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| High | 7 | Strong named-product or direct 2026 rate support. |
| Medium-high | 20 | Multiple credible anchors with manageable comparability differences. |
| Medium | 16 | Usable as a provisional editorial band; validate against one live representative itinerary before launch. |
| Medium-low | 9 | Dynamic/quote-led hotel markets or broad categories; do not launch without supplier confirmation. |
| Low-medium | 2 | Event and multigenerational normalization is structurally weak; rebuild on duration/party composition first. |

Highest analytical risks:

- **Broad-region pages:** Africa, Asia, Europe, the Middle East, North America, South America, and the South Pacific aggregate markets with very different lodging, air, and guide economics.
- **Occupancy mismatch:** family and multigenerational travel cannot be represented reliably by the default two-adult double-share basis; child ages, room count, and party size must be explicit.
- **Voyage duration:** Galápagos is normalized to seven nights and event trips to three–five nights. Polar/expedition pages need a visible typical duration beside any public range.
- **Permit separation:** Rwanda’s $1,500 gorilla permit, Galápagos’s $200 levy and $20 TCT, and other destination fees must remain visibly included or excluded; never leave their treatment implicit.
