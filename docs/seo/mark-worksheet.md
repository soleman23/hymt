# Everything the site needs from Mark — one sitting

Prepared 2026-08-09 for the launch of www.hymtravel.com. Four parts. Nothing
here needs research beyond your own records and memory; anything you cannot
verify, leave blank — **a blank ships nothing, a guess ships a lie.** Partial
returns are welcome; we insert what you fill and the rest waits.

Fill this in any way that suits you — type between the lines, annotate a
printout, or dictate it in page order. Send it back to Devin however is easiest.

**Ground rules that apply throughout**
- Ranges, not quotes. A low and a high for how you actually plan that trip.
- Never estimate a flight time or a figure. Blank beats wrong.
- Date anything perishable — every filled cost row gets "verified as of".
- First-hand lines must be genuinely yours: a month and year, a named
  property, one concrete observation. If you have not been, say so and name
  the on-the-ground partner — that ships too, and reads stronger than silence.
