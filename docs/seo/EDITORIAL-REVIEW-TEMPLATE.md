# Editorial review record

Use one copy per destination, experience, or journal page before a substantive
release. The public repository may hold this blank template and non-sensitive
status. Keep private evidence and permissions in the owner's restricted records.
An empty row or `review required` is not approval.

## Material claim log

Record a separate row for each claim about access, cost, timing, safety,
credentials, first-hand experience, client results, or supplier benefits.
Avoid private client details in this file.

| Exact claim / page section | Type: Mark firsthand, permitted client work, supplier, or independent research | Evidence URL or private ID | Scope: traveler, place, date/season, price currency | Checked on | Named reviewer / reviewed on | Next review or earlier trigger | Decision / correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | | | | Review required |

**Additional checks for changing facts**

- Price or tax: identify whether per person, per room, per night, inclusive of
  fees/taxes, and the booking/travel window. Verify against a current quote.
- Event access or conservation permit: identify issuing authority, inventory,
  eligibility, and the date the rule or price was checked.
- Visa, entry, and health: link the traveler to the relevant official authority;
  record nationality and route assumptions. Do not substitute a general site
  statement for individual medical or legal advice.
- Seasonal transport: name the operator and route, operating dates, timetable
  source, and weather or maintenance caveat.
- Firsthand: Mark confirms the place, timing, and specific observation. Record
  the confirmation date and private evidence ID. Without it, remove or rewrite
  the first-person claim before approving the page.
- Client work: record the exact approved wording, consent date, and private
  permission ID. Remove identifying details that are not explicitly permitted.

Generated images must not be used as proof that Mark visited a location or
that a depicted room, access benefit, wildlife scene, or client experience is
real. Prefer an original or licensed real replacement when available. Preserve
the new image's source file, license, credit terms, and crop history.
