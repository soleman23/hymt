# HYMT monthly newsletter plan: November 2026 – October 2027

One email a month from Mark to the Reach list, built in Claude Design and sent
through Hostinger Reach. This file is the plan. `claude-design-kit.md` beside it
holds the prompts you paste into Claude Design to produce each finished email.

Written 2026-10-25, working from the ten-month plan Mark emailed (November 2026 to
August 2027) and adding September and October 2027.

---

## Who it's for, and what each email has to do

- **The list is mostly past clients.** They already trust Mark, so the emails
  don't need to sell him. Each one needs to put a specific trip idea in front of
  them early enough that they still have their pick of dates and rooms.
- **The main ask is "reply to this email."** For a past client, replying is the
  easiest next step. Every email also has one button to Plan Your Trip for
  people who prefer a form.
- **The secondary ask is referrals.** Past clients are the referral base, so a
  one-line P.S. asks for one every quarter (see the calendar).
- **Every email sells a trip 6–18 months out, never next month's.** The original
  plan already did this, and it's the plan's best feature. Keep it.

## What changed from the original plan

1. **Each email follows the same set of blocks** (below). Claude Design then
   fills in one template each month instead of designing from scratch, and
   readers learn where to look.
2. **Each email has a "Book-by" box.** It's one dated, specific line about
   timing, such as "For February 2027 travel, decide by Thanksgiving." This is
   where an advisor earns the email, and it gives a past client a reason to
   reply now instead of someday.
3. **Mark never claims he took a trip himself.** "Mark's Take" is advice: what he
   asks clients, what people get wrong, what he'd prioritize. Never "when I
   stayed at…". This applies to every email.
4. **Each email links a Travel Journal article along with the experience page.**
   The site already has articles that match most months (*Safari Planning: 12
   Questions*, *The European Grand Tour Mistake*, *Kyoto in April vs.
   November* and others). The original plan didn't use them.
5. **March has concrete destinations.** "Guided walking" needs places to point
   at: Patagonia, the Galápagos, the Dolomites and the Canadian Rockies all have
   pages.
6. **August has a 2028 hook: the LA28 Summer Olympics (July 2028).** It's the
   biggest US sports event of 2028, and a past client asking about it in August
   2027 is right on time.
7. **September and October are new.**
   - **September 2027 is Japan.** Asia was the plan's biggest gap. *Kyoto in
     April vs. November* already says cherry-blossom season books the best
     ryokan twelve months out, so a September 2027 send lands exactly a year
     before.
   - **October 2027 is "Your 2028 travel calendar: what to book when."** It
     marks the newsletter's first year, recaps the year's topics in one
     scannable list and asks readers what they want next year. That reply
     list becomes next year's plan.
8. **There's a fixed send day:** the first Thursday of the month at 8:00 am
   Pacific, moved where a holiday gets in the way. The 2026 US midterm election
   is Tuesday, November 3, so November goes out on the 5th.

## Voice rules

- **Specifics over adjectives:** "Grace Bay's 12 miles of beach" beats
  "stunning beaches."
- **No personal trip claims.** Mark speaks as the advisor: "The question I ask
  first is…", "What people usually get wrong is…", "If it were my booking, I'd
  lock in…". Never "When I was in…" or "My favorite night was…".
- **No prices, deals or "limited time"** unless Mark gives a dated, confirmed
  offer for that month.
- **Every timing claim gets checked before sending.** The Book-by lines in the
  kit are drafts based on how these trips usually book. Mark confirms each one
  with his suppliers the week of the send.
- **Event and ticket emails (August) never imply HYMT sells admission.** Mark
  arranges travel, stays and authorized hospitality packages. Tickets come only
  through official channels.
- **No "Lorem ipsum", "TBD" or placeholder images ever go out.**

## Operations

### Building each email (about 45 minutes a month)

1. Around the 20th of the month before, paste that month's brief from
   `claude-design-kit.md` into Claude Design, along with the master prompt.
2. Mark reads the draft. He changes anything that doesn't sound like him and
   confirms the Book-by line.
3. Export the HTML. Paste it into Reach's **Code editor**. Don't use the
   template API, which skips Reach's fix-up pass.
4. In the Reach campaign wizard, set the subject and the **preheader**. Reach
   does not read the preheader from the HTML.
5. Send a test to Gmail on desktop, the iPhone Mail app and Outlook. Check that
   every link works, the images load and the unsubscribe link works.
6. Schedule it for 8:00 am Pacific on the send date.
7. Save the final HTML in this folder as `YYYY-MM-<slug>.html`, the same way as
   `2026-09-new-website-announcement.html`, so there's always a copy that
   doesn't depend on Claude Design.

### What Reach breaks (learned on the September email)

- Reach turns side-by-side columns into 50% table cells with no mobile
  breakpoint, so they stay side by side on phones. **Single column only.**
- Reach strips the doctype, comments, `role` attributes and Outlook (MSO)
  conditional code.
- **No `<style>` block, no web fonts, no scripts, no embedded data-URI
  images.** All CSS is inline and all images are hosted.
- The footer must end with Reach's own unsubscribe block (the
  `{{unsubscribe-link}}` merge tag), copied verbatim from the September email.

### Images

- Hosted on the site at `https://www.hymtravel.com/assets/img/`, added through
  the repo's `images-b64` pipeline like September's.
- File names follow `nl-YYYY-MM-<slug>-<what>.jpg`, e.g.
  `nl-2026-11-winter-escape-hero.jpg`. Use a new name every time and never
  overwrite an old one, so cached copies can't show the wrong image.
- Prepare them at 2× display size: the hero at 1200 px wide, cards at 1088 px.
- Every image gets alt text.

### Link tracking

Every link to hymtravel.com carries
`?utm_source=reach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=nl-YYYY-MM-<slug>`.
Example: `utm_campaign=nl-2026-11-winter-escape`. GA4 then shows exactly which
email produced each Plan Your Trip submission.

### What to measure monthly

Open rate is unreliable because Apple Mail Privacy Protection pre-opens
emails. Track these four instead:

1. **Click rate.** Healthy is 2–5% for a small list of past clients.
2. **Replies.** Count them by hand. This is the number that matters most.
3. **Plan Your Trip submissions** with `utm_source=reach` (from GA4).
4. **Unsubscribes.** More than 0.5% on one send means that topic missed.

   valid postal address (a street address, PO box or registered private mailbox)
   in every marketing email. The September footer only says "Bend, Oregon." Which
   address should go there?
2. **The site's signup copy contradicts a monthly newsletter.** After someone

   one-line site change. Say the word and I'll make it.

   someone has to copy each one across by hand. Either do that once a month
   before the send, or connect the form to Reach. The latter needs the Zapier
   or Hostinger connector authorized.
4. **A welcome email for new subscribers (recommended).** If Mark's Reach plan
   includes automations, a one-time welcome email ("what to expect, and reply
   anytime") should go to every new subscriber. It can be written in the same

   The design tools move into Claude itself. November and December can be built
