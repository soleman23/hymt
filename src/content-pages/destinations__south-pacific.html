A Starting Point for Your Brief

Match the Route to Your Priorities

These are planning scenarios, not bookable itineraries or fixed minimum stays. Compare them with your departure airport, party and travel window before reserving hotels.

One Island Base

Start with: Fiji or one resort base in French Polynesia.

Best fit: A couple seeking downtime, or a family that prefers unpacking once. Verify children's ages, room occupancy and any childcare arrangements with the chosen resort.

Tradeoff: Fewer hotel moves, less variety. A remote island can still require a boat or flight; one hotel does not mean one transfer.

Next decision: Would a beach room meet your needs, or is a particular villa, view or pool essential?

Two Islands in One Country

Start with: Tahiti and Moorea, with Bora Bora considered separately; or Rarotonga and Aitutaki.

Best fit: Travelers who want a different setting partway through the holiday and accept a moving day.

Tradeoff: More variety, another check-out and connection. Tahiti–Moorea has a ferry option; Rarotonga–Aitutaki requires a flight. Verify the timetable before choosing hotel dates.

Next decision: Is the second island worth the transfer time, baggage handling and extra cost?

An Active Country Route

Start with: One island of New Zealand or a focused regional route in Australia.

Best fit: Travelers who want walks, food, cities or landscapes to shape the day. Discuss driving comfort, walking ability and recovery time before adding bases.

Tradeoff: Greater day-to-day variety, more road or flight time. A country route needs accommodation at several stops rather than a single resort reservation.

Next decision: Which two or three places matter enough to justify moving? Leave the rest for another trip.

A Country Route Plus an Island Stay

Start with: An Australia or New Zealand route, then assess an island stay against the available international connections.

Best fit: Travelers who want activity followed by downtime and have room in the calendar for the additional journey.

Tradeoff: Another international flight and potentially a transit night. Compare this with staying longer in the first country; do not assume every island has a direct connection.

Next decision: Would you rather use the extra days at your existing bases? A second country should earn its place.

Connection references: Tourism Fiji sea transfers, Tahiti Tourisme domestic flights and Moorea ferries, and Cook Islands flight guidance. These describe transport options, not confirmed connections for your trip.

Choose Dates by Destination

There Is No Single South Pacific Season

Fiji and French Polynesia

Tourism Fiji and Tahiti Tourisme describe May–October as the drier part of the year. November–April is generally warmer and wetter. Rain and wind remain possible in either period.

For a holiday built around the water, check the chosen island and activity with the operator. A broad seasonal label does not settle diving conditions, boat departures or the best side of a resort.

Australia and New Zealand

Tourism New Zealand identifies December–February as summer. Tourism Australia distinguishes the tropical north's May–October dry season from southern Australia's four seasons.

A southern summer route and a northern reef or rainforest route need different date checks. Choose the activities first, then verify regional conditions and access for the month you can travel.

For the Cook Islands and Vanuatu, use the specific country pages and current local operator advice. Do not apply Fiji's seasonal pattern to every island. Climate references checked October 4, 2026; recheck access and timetables before booking.

Build a Comparable Budget

Price the Whole Route, Not Just the Room

Compare quotes for the same departure airport, dates, travelers and room requirements. A single nightly price cannot represent a Fiji resort stay, a Bora Bora villa and a New Zealand road trip.

Keep the assumptions identical

  • Departure airport, flight cabin and total nights away
  • Number of adults, children's ages and required rooms
  • Room or villa category, bedding and accessibility needs
  • Meal plan, included activities and cancellation terms

Ask for each cost line

  • International flights and any domestic or onward flights
  • Airport, ferry, boat or resort transfers and transit nights
  • Accommodation, meals, activities and any vehicle hire
  • Taxes, service charges, currency and quote expiry date

Treat a one-base stay, a two-island stay and a country-plus-island route as separate quotes, with optional upgrades itemized. Transfer guidance from Tourism Fiji and Tahiti Tourisme helps identify the journeys to include; it does not establish an all-in price. Ask for current rates for your party and dates.
