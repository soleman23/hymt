About This Journal

Written from experience, not research

Every piece in this journal is based on time actually spent in the places we're writing about — not press trips, not aggregated research, not AI-generated summaries of destinations we've never visited. If it's in here, someone from Hit Your Mark has been there.

We write about timing, access, what the mainstream coverage gets wrong, and which experiences are worth the premium. The goal is to help you travel more intelligently — whether you book with us or not.
