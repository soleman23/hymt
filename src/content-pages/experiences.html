The Masters —
What Augusta Actually Is

Augusta National does not sell tickets. It issues patron badges, and the waiting list for them closed in 1978 and has never reopened. There are no visible sponsors anywhere on the 365 acres, phones stay off the course, and the pimento cheese sandwich still costs $1.50.

Practice-round access runs through a public lottery that opens each June for the following April. Tournament rounds move through the patron network instead, where single-day weekend badges have regularly sold in the $3,000–$8,000 range. Mark's field report walks both paths, and what the course is actually like once you're inside the gates.
