Playing a famous course and watching a championship on one are two different trips. Playing is a question of access: a ballot, a resort stay, a handicap certificate, a home-club verification. Watching is a question of tickets and dates. This page is about playing, with a week at The Open as the one exception. For every other sport and event, start with sports and event travel.

The order of the rounds matters as much as the list. On links courses the wind decides how hard a day is, so the round that matters most goes early in the week, with a spare day behind it. Most travellers play better with a rest day after every two or three rounds. A partner who does not play needs a plan of their own, not a seat in the cart.

Before You Commit

Who a Golf Trip Suits, and Who It Does Not

You do not need to be an expert. You do need a handicap, and the stamina to walk.

⬡
Right for: the club golfer
The Old Course asks for a handicap of 36 or lower, on a certificate or record from a recognised golf association, per the St Andrews Links Trust. That is a limit most regular club golfers already meet.
⬡
Right for: members of a club
Private clubs that take visitors check membership. Royal Melbourne asks international visitors' home clubs to verify them as active members before a tee time, per its visitor page.
⬡
Not right for: anyone who will not walk
Links golf is played on foot, often in wind and rain, and many golfers take a caddie rather than a cart. Several rounds in a week is a physical trip, not a resort holiday with golf on the side.
⬡
Not right for: a non-golfer, unplanned
A partner who does not play will spend five hours alone each round day. It works only when their days are planned as carefully as the tee times.

St Andrews · Fife

How to Get onto the Old Course

Rules from the St Andrews Links Trust, checked October 2026.

The Old Course is public, and there are two routes onto it. The first is an advance tee time, sold through application windows the year before the season. For 2027, one window, for eligible American Express Platinum and Centurion members, ran from 26 August to 9 September 2026, per the Links Trust's application page.

The second is the daily ballot, a draw for tee times with results posted two days before play. A traveller who arrives without an advance time enters the ballot each eligible day and builds the week so a successful draw can be played. The Old Course is closed for play on Sundays, and for further periods through the year for course commitments, per the Old Course page. Every player must show a handicap of 36 or lower on documentation from a recognised golf association or union.

The Old Course is one of seven courses at St Andrews Links, which describes itself as the largest public golf complex in Europe; the New, Jubilee, Eden, Strathtyrum, Balgove and Castle courses fill the rest of the week, per the Links Trust. Licensed caddies can be requested at any time before a tee time, and more notice improves the chance of one, per the Old Course booking page. For where to stay and what else to see, start with the UK and Ireland.

The Open · July 2027

Watching The Open, and Playing the Same Week

The 155th Open is at St Andrews from 11 to 18 July 2027, per The R&A, checked October 2026.

The Open moves between links venues in Scotland and England, and The R&A names each venue years ahead. In 2027 it returns to St Andrews. The week has two halves. Monday to Wednesday are practice days: the Last-Chance Qualifier on Monday 12 July, the Heroes Classic on Tuesday 13 July, and the players' final preparations on Wednesday 14 July. The Championship itself runs Thursday 15 to Sunday 18 July.

Practice days are the easier days to watch: fewer people, players close at hand, and time to walk the course. Championship days are the reason most people come. The public ticket ballot for 2027 has closed; The R&A's tickets and hospitality page lists the routes still open, including Ticket Plus and hospitality packages.

To play in the same week, plan the rounds on other courses: the Old Course is the Championship venue that week. The usual shape is rounds on nearby links on the days either side of the days spent watching, with the Links Trust's other courses or the coast beyond Fife filling the gaps.

Augusta · April

The Masters

Augusta National is a private club, so the Masters is a trip to watch, not to play. Augusta National allocates practice-round tickets and a limited number of daily tournament tickets through one online application a year, and the deadline for 2027 was 20 June 2026, per its ticket FAQ. Everything else is in the Masters patron badges and practice-round access report.
