The Approach

How I think about travel — and why it matters

Four things I believe about luxury travel that most agencies won't tell you.

01 — Warm
Travel is personal. It should feel that way.

The best trips don't come from an algorithm or a template. They come from a real conversation about what you actually want — the feeling you're after, not just the destination. I ask questions most agents don't think to ask, and I listen to the answers.

02 — Confident
You don't need to be sold to. You need to be guided.

Luxury travelers are intelligent people making considered decisions. I don't pitch, I don't oversell, and I don't manufacture urgency. I tell you what I know, what I'd recommend, and why — and I let you decide.

03 — Editorial
The details are the trip.

Which side of the caldera. Which villa on which cliff. Which guide has been here thirty years versus which one just got certified. These are not small details — they are the difference between a trip you remember and one you merely completed.

04 — Never Salesy
The invitation stands. There's no deadline.

There is no "limited availability." There is no "book before Friday." When you're ready to plan something exceptional, I'm here. The right trip is worth taking the time to get right. That's not a sales tactic — it's just the truth.
