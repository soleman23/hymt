What It Costs

What an event trip Actually Costs

Priced by the seat, not by the night
What sets the number here is the seat rather than the room, and a seat is priced per event, per day and per person's age — six sports on this page span a range no single nightly figure could sit inside.
What actually sets the number
  • Which event — the ticket, not the room, is the largest line on the trip
  • Party composition — Monaco prices ages six to fifteen at half the adult ticket, and free on Thursday
  • Duration — a two- or three-day Monaco package takes 10% off the daily rate; a single Sunday does not

Tell me the dates and the party and you get a real quote, not a range. Source: Automobile Club de Monaco ticket terms.
