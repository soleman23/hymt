Why Sports Travel

More Than a Ticket
to the Game

Marquee events run on 6–18 month lead times for the access worth having: Formula 1, Wimbledon, the Masters, World Cup matches. Some inventory releases later when earlier packages fall through, but the good seats are rarely in it. The event fixes the dates, so the rest of the trip is built around a fixed point rather than the other way round.

Anyone can buy a ticket. What we arrange is the experience around it — the suite at a five-star hotel walking distance from the venue, the private transfer that gets you there without the traffic, the dinner reservation at the restaurant where the players eat after the match.

The sport is the centrepiece. Everything else is what makes it a trip worth talking about. We have deep relationships with hospitality providers at every major event on the calendar — access that isn't available on public ticketing platforms, and logistics that make the chaos disappear.

Whether you're planning a once-in-a-lifetime bucket-list event or an annual pilgrimage to the same tournament, we build the journey around the sport and wrap it in the level of travel you expect from Hit Your Mark.
