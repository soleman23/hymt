The conventional advice is to visit the Mediterranean in June or September. Both are reasonable. October is the month I suggest most often when a client's dates are flexible — but only with a clear answer to two questions: which part of the Mediterranean, and which half of the month. The sea does not cool, the ferries do not thin and the hotels do not close at the same pace everywhere.

The short answer

October works best in the eastern Mediterranean and in the first half of the month. Antalya's long-term October average high is 26.6°C, against 23.9°C in Palma, and Dubrovnik averages 125 mm of October rain — more than in September. Go east for warmth and swimming. Go to Italy's countryside for food and walking. Confirm ferries and hotels for any dates after mid-month.

"October is not one month across the Mediterranean. It is a warm east, a cooling west, and a second half that runs on fewer boats."

East or west: what the climate records show

National weather services publish long-term monthly averages, and they make the split plain. In Antalya, on Turkey's southern coast, October averages a 26.6°C daily high and about five days with rain. In Palma de Mallorca, the October average high is 23.9°C, with about six rain days of 1 mm or more. In Dubrovnik, October's average temperature is 18.0°C and its rainfall, 125 mm, is higher than September's 96 mm, with roughly 50 fewer hours of sunshine.

What that means for a trip: the eastern Mediterranean holds summer longer, so it suits a trip where swimming and long days outdoors are the point. The western Mediterranean and the Adriatic shift sooner toward autumn, which is no hardship for cities, wine country and walking, but it is the wrong bet for a beach week. These are averages, not forecasts. Any given October can run warm or wet, and the sea can be rough enough to cancel boats on a sunny day.

The sea itself holds warmth into October, but the figure varies by place and year. On the Catalan coast, for example, the sea at L'Estartit measured 21.7°C on October 8, 2025 — 1.8°C above its 1998–2022 average for that date. Treat swimming as likely in the east and early in the month, and as a bonus elsewhere.

Early or late October: where each half works

The bigger change inside the month is not the weather but the infrastructure. Ferry timetables, island hotels and beach clubs are built around summer, and many wind down toward November.

The tradeoffs to plan around

Ferries. In the Cyclades, indicative schedules show a route such as Piraeus to Folegandros running six to seven days a week in October and three to five in November. On the Amalfi Coast, ferries generally run from April through October; for 2026, some NLG routes from Positano are listed through November 3, while the Alicost weekend service to Naples is listed through October 31. Every operator warns that sailings can be suspended in rough seas. For late October, choose islands and towns you can also reach by air or road.

Closures. Seasonal island hotels, beach clubs and some village restaurants close in late October or reduce their days. This varies property by property, so the rule is simple: confirm what is open for your exact dates before you book the rest of the trip around it.

Rain and wind. October brings more rain days than September across most of the region, and in the Adriatic noticeably more. A day lost to weather matters more on an island than in a city, which is another reason to keep late-October island stays short.

Trip length. With fewer boats, every transfer takes more of the day. For a ten-night October trip, I would hold to two or three bases rather than the four or five a summer itinerary might attempt.

Booking lead time. October is quieter than summer, not empty. The best-located rooms at the smaller hotels, and private guides or estate visits in harvest weeks, still go early. Start planning in spring for an October trip.

Food and harvest in October

Italy's countryside is the strongest argument for October. Truffle-hunting season is under way in Tuscany, and the San Miniato area's village fairs begin in October — the 2026 calendar lists Balconevisi on October 17–18 and La Serra from October 30 to November 1. The main San Miniato white truffle market follows on three weekends in November, from the 14th to the 29th in 2026. In Puglia, the olive harvest usually begins in late October and peaks in November. Grape harvest dates vary with the year and the estate, so treat it as something to ask about rather than to plan the trip on.

Two sample October routes

These are planning shapes, not priced itineraries. Each assumes ten nights and needs current schedules and hotel availability checked before booking.

Italy, first half of October — food, cities and one coastal stay. Rome for three nights, the Tuscan countryside for four, then the Amalfi Coast for three. The coast goes last while ferries are still on their full season, and the order can be reversed if swimming matters more than the countryside. See Italy for the regions and pace, and our food and wine travel page for estate visits.

Athens and the islands, or the Turkish coast — any time in October. Athens for two nights, then one Cycladic island with good year-round connections for five, and back to Athens for the final three or onward travel. In late October, swap the island for Turkey's Turquoise Coast, where the climate record runs warmest, by land or on a gulet charter, after confirming the operator's season. See Greece for island choices.

For Croatia, keep to Dubrovnik and Split rather than an island circuit, and consider a small-ship cruise only after checking which ports it still calls at. Spain and Portugal follow the western pattern: excellent for cities, wine and walking, less reliable for the beach.

My recommendation

If your dates are flexible, pick the first half of October and decide what the trip is for. If it is the sea, go east. If it is food, wine and towns, Italy and Spain are at their best. If your dates fall in the second half, build the trip on places that do not depend on a ferry. How we build October itineraries

The first question is whether the dates can move. Then I check, for those exact dates, which hotels are open, which ferry or flight connections are running, and where the weather record favors the plan. The route follows from what is operating, not from the summer version of the trip.

Where October Is Best Spent

Italy in October is the harvest argument — truffle fairs in Tuscany, the start of the olive harvest in Puglia, and quieter cities.

Greece holds summer longer in the first half of the month, and Spain is at its best for cities, wine regions and walking.
