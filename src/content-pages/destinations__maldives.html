Why the Maldives

Not Every Overwater Villa
Is the Same

The photographs are accurate. The water is that colour, the villas do sit over it, and the silence in the early morning is as complete as it looks. What the photographs can't show is the difference between an atoll with a functioning house reef forty steps from your deck and one where the marine life has been grazed to nothing.

We know which atolls are worth the longer seaplane transfer, which properties have maintained their reef, and which ones have trading primarily on legacy reputation. Those distinctions are not available on a booking site. They require time in the water — which we've put in.

Most clients ask about overwater villas. We ask which direction the villa faces, whether the prevailing wind will be in your face on the deck, and what the lagoon depth is at low tide. That's the difference between a beautiful room and a trip you'll spend the next decade recommending.

When to Go

The Maldives Season by Season

Dry Season
Nov – Apr
Clear skies, calm seas, excellent visibility for diving. The benchmark season — book 9–12 months ahead for the best properties.
Best Time to Go
Shoulder
May – Jun
Weather begins to soften. Some rain, but often afternoons only. Rates drop meaningfully and resorts operate at lower occupancy.
Wet Season
Jul – Sep
Heavier rainfall and rougher surf on west-facing atolls. The Manta season. Best for experienced divers; not ideal for first-timers.
Transition
Oct – Nov
Weather improves as the season shifts. Fewer crowds, negotiable rates. Often an underrated window for couples who've been before.
What to Do

Experiences Worth Planning Around

The Maldives isn't one destination — it's an archipelago of 1,200 islands spread across 90,000 square kilometres of Indian Ocean. What you do depends entirely on which atoll you're in.

01
House Reef Diving & Snorkelling
The best atolls have a functioning reef directly accessible from your villa. Hammerheads at Fuvahmulah, mantas at Baa Atoll, and whale sharks year-round at South Ari. We match you to the atoll that fits what you're actually after.
02
Private Sandbank Dinners
A table for two on a uninhabited sandbank. Champagne chilling in the shallows, a chef setting up on the sand, no other guests for miles. This is standard at the better properties — and the detail that distinguishes a stay from an experience.
03
Sunset Dolphin Cruises
Spinner dolphins are resident in most atolls and reliably active at dusk. A private dhoni with an experienced guide, a cooler of wine, and an Indian Ocean sunset. It doesn't require much more than that.
04
Underwater Spa Treatments
Several properties in North Malé Atoll operate glass-walled treatment rooms below the lagoon surface. Massage with manta rays gliding past the window is the kind of detail that sounds absurd until you've experienced it.
05
Island-Hopping by Seaplane
The aerial view alone justifies the transfer. But combining two or three atolls in one trip — a diving atoll and a seclusion atoll — is how experienced Maldives travelers structure a two-week stay rather than one long week in one place.
06
Private Chef Experiences
Beyond the standard in-villa dining, several properties arrange private chef residencies — a dedicated cook sourcing locally caught tuna and tropical fruit, preparing meals around your preferences rather than a fixed menu. Available by arrangement.

Common Questions

Maldives — FAQ

Questions about choosing the right resort, what is included, seaplane logistics, and who the Maldives is actually right for.

Each resort prices differently. A typical package covers accommodation, breakfast, non-motorized water sports, and transfers from the local island airport. Premium drinks, spa treatments, scuba diving, and most excursions are usually extra. All-inclusive rates are available at most resorts and make sense if you plan to eat and drink extensively on-property.

Key variables: location relative to Male international airport (some resorts are 30 minutes by speedboat; others need a 40-minute seaplane), reef quality for snorkeling and diving, food quality, overwater villa configuration, and service culture. The gap between a $600/night and $2,000/night Maldives resort is real. I visit properties personally and advise based on what each client actually prioritizes.

No. While it excels for honeymoons and anniversaries, the Maldives works for anyone seeking total relaxation, exceptional marine environments, and resort seclusion. Families visit regularly, and a number of resorts have strong children's programming and shallow lagoons ideal for young swimmers. The experience scales to who is traveling.

November through April is the dry season — the peak window with the most consistent weather and best visibility for snorkeling and diving. May–October is wetter, with potentially rough seas, but significantly lower rates. Many clients travel in shoulder months (late April or October–November) for a balance of conditions and value.

No. Snorkeling with a life jacket provides access to extraordinary marine life at virtually every resort — reef sharks, manta rays, sea turtles, and hundreds of fish species. Non-swimmers can fully enjoy the overwater villa experience, spa, sunset cruises, and sandbank excursions. Scuba adds depth, but it is not required for a meaningful trip.

Many resorts in remote atolls require a 20–40 minute seaplane transfer in a Twin Otter floatplane. Seaplanes operate in daylight hours only, meaning late-arriving international flights may require a night's transit accommodation in Male. I factor this into every Maldives itinerary and book transfer-compatible international connections to avoid unnecessary transit nights.

The combination of water clarity, marine life access, overwater villa experience, and genuine resort seclusion is difficult to replicate elsewhere. The best Maldives properties deliver a level of service and environment that justifies the price for travelers who value these things. For travelers who find pure relaxation less satisfying than cultural exploration, the cost-to-experience ratio may be less compelling.
