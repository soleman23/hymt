The Willamette Valley,
Oregon's Answer to Burgundy

In 1979 a French critic, tasting blind, mistook an Oregon Pinot Noir for a top Burgundy. Joseph Drouhin flew over to investigate, bought land, and planted. Fifty years on, most American wine travelers still haven't been.

One hour from Portland, across the Dundee Hills and the Chehalem Mountains. This is the route worth doing while it still feels like a discovery.
